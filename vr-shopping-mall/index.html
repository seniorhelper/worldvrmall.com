A VR shopping mall that opens in your headset browser

Put on a Meta Quest (or any headset with a WebXR browser), go to worldvrmall.com and tap Enter VR. The same mall that runs on phones becomes a place you stand inside, with hands, a laser and a wrist menu.

No store listing, no download

World VR Mall is a website built on WebXR, the open standard headset browsers use for immersive pages. There is no app to approve or update. The address you share with a friend on a phone is the same address that opens the mall in a headset.

What VR mode gives you

ControlWhat it does

Comfort options are built in: a vignette while moving, snap turn, teleport, and rides that keep you at seated eye height.

Shopping in a headset, honestly

Browsing in VR is slower than scrolling a list, and we don't pretend otherwise. That is why every store also has a plain page in the Directory, and why checkout always happens on the brand's own site. Use the headset for the part that is better in VR: walking in, looking around, and finding things you weren't searching for.

How to get in

  • Phone or tablet: open worldvrmall.com, drag to look, use the joystick or tap where you want to walk.
  • Laptop or desktop: W-A-S-D or the arrow keys, drag the mouse to look.
  • VR headset (Meta Quest and other WebXR browsers): open the same address in the headset browser and tap Enter VR.
  • No 3D at all: the Mall feed and the Directory are plain pages with every store, product and phone number.
🛠️ Still being built. World VR Mall opened in 2026 and grows every week. Some corners look rough, especially in headsets. If something breaks, tell us; a person reads every report.

Questions

Which headsets work?

Headsets with a WebXR browser. We build and test on Meta Quest 3. Others with WebXR support should open it too; tell us if yours has trouble.

Do I need a Meta or Steam app?

No. Open worldvrmall.com in the headset's web browser and tap Enter VR.

Can I buy in VR?

Choose products in VR. The buy button leaves VR cleanly and opens the brand's own website to finish the purchase.

Does it work seated?

Yes. Rides keep you at seated eye height, and you can turn with the stick instead of your body.
