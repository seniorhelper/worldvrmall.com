3. How to play

The Prize Hunt is a game of skill: explore World VR Mall in a web browser, free, and find the prizes hidden in the 3D world. When you find one, a claim form opens. Submit the form with accurate information. You may also claim by calling 1-800-481-8638 if the form does not work for you, using the claim code shown on screen.

Each prize goes to the first eligible person who finds it and submits a valid, complete claim. Limit one prize per person and per household for the whole promotion.
