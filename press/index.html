Boilerplate

World VR Mall (worldvrmall.com) is a free 3D virtual shopping mall built in Denver, Colorado and open to the world. Independent stores and brands have walk-in storefronts with buy buttons to their own checkout; visitors explore on phones, laptops, or VR headsets with no app to install. The grounds include three roller coasters, a Sky Wheel, a lake, an old-world market for makers, a train and a kids' discovery zone. Brands can rent a storefront month to month from $99. Contact: 1-800-481-8638.
