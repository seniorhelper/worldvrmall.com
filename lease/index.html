Frequently asked questions

How much does a store in World VR Mall cost?

Stores are a monthly lease. Pricing depends on placement (lobby, wing, food court, or market booth) and store size, so it's a ten-minute phone call rather than a price list. Call 1-800-481-8638.

Do I need to build anything?

No. You send a logo, your brand colors, and a product list with links to where people buy. We build the storefront, the AI clerk, and your store page. Setup takes about ten minutes on the phone and your store is usually live the same day.

Who handles the payments?

You do. Every buy button links to your own checkout: Shopify, Square, Etsy, WooCommerce, or any site. World VR Mall never touches your money or your customer data.

What is premium placement?

The storefronts every visitor walks past: the Grand Lobby and the first stores in each wing. They are limited to the doors that exist and go first come, first served.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Leases are month to month.
