3. Your selfie and your data

  • Selfies: if you are not signed in, the photo you take for your avatar is processed in your browser and saved only on your device. If you sign in with an allofus.one account (adults 18+, account at least 3 days old) and choose to use your selfie face, a small copy is uploaded and checked by an automated safety scanner (Hive) before anyone else can see it. Pictures that fail the check are never shown and are kept privately for our safety team. You can remove your face any time from the character screen.
  • Date of birth: accounts give a date of birth. It is private, never shown, and is used only to confirm you are 18+ for live social and prize eligibility.
  • Age check (when switched on): voice, live video and The World Fire will need a one-time age check run by a specialist partner (Yoti, or Persona as a back-up): a quick selfie age estimate with a liveness check, or an ID document and selfie match if the estimate is close. The partner shows its own notice and consent screen. We receive and keep only the result (passed or not), the method, an age band and the partner's reference number, never your selfie, video or ID.
  • Shopping list, bag, coins, and character settings are likewise stored only on your device.
  • Contact forms: if you send a message to a tenant through a form in the mall, the name, phone, email, and message you type are delivered to that tenant so they can reply. Don't include anything you wouldn't put in an email.
  • Analytics: we may use standard, aggregate website analytics (page views, device type, approximate region) to understand how the mall is used. We do not sell personal information.
  • Embedded media: videos, radio stations, and the NASA map are embedded from YouTube and NASA. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Live features and how we keep them safe

The mall is open to everyone with no account. Live social (seeing other people live, offering hugs, messages, voice, live video and the gathering room) is only for signed-in adults 18+ with an allofus.one account. These protections are always on:

  • Adults only, enforced by our servers. Guests, anyone under 18 and anyone not signed in as an adult explore on their own and never see or hear live people; our servers never send them anyone's position, voice or messages. A member reported as possibly under 18 is hidden from live social until they pass an age check.
  • Voice is off until you turn it on, works push-to-talk (the microphone is live only while you hold the button), and only reaches people standing near you. Audio goes directly between browsers and is never recorded. Voice and live video will need the one-time age check described above once it is switched on.
  • Hugs need both people to say yes. Nobody can walk into you: every avatar keeps a personal-space bubble around you.
  • Mute, block or report anyone by tapping them (or pointing at them in VR). Blocked people vanish for each other. A real person reviews every report, and the person you report is not told who reported them. Several reports mute an account automatically until we review it; a child-safety report puts the account on hold at once.
  • No phone numbers, emails, links, social handles or money talk in anything players can see, so nobody can move a conversation off World VR Mall. Names that pretend to be our staff are not allowed. Buy buttons only go to verified tenant websites; any other link shows a warning first.
  • Evidence. When something is reported we keep a secure record of what happened (who, where, when, and the hugs or messages involved) so our team, and where the law requires it the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children or the police, can act on it.

Prize claims and store or investor inquiries you submit are stored securely so our team can respond. The Prize Hunt rules and the Community Rules apply: family friendly, always. If a child is in danger, call 911. To report child sexual exploitation, you can also use the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.
