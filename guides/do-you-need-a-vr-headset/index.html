Do you need a VR headset to shop in a virtual mall?

No. A browser-based 3D mall works on a phone or laptop. Here is what each device gets you, which browsers support VR, and how to try it in a minute.

Short answer

No. A browser-based 3D mall like World VR Mall works on any recent phone, tablet or laptop with no app. A VR headset is optional: open the same web address in the headset's browser and you can walk the mall in VR, using a web standard called WebXR.

"Virtual mall" sounds like something you need special gear for. You don't. You need a web browser. This guide covers what each device gets you, which browsers can do the VR part, and the quickest way to try it.

How does VR work in a web browser?

VR in a browser uses the WebXR Device API, a web standard that lets a page ask the headset for its position and controllers and draw a 3D scene for each eye (MDN Web Docs, WebXR Device API). MDN marks it "limited availability" because it doesn't work in some of the most widely used browsers. The page checks whether an immersive VR session is available and only then shows an "Enter VR" option. Nothing is installed; close the tab and it's gone.

Which browsers support it?

On November 22, 2026, Can I use: WebXR listed partial support in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Samsung Internet, while desktop Safari and Firefox have it turned off by default and Safari on iPhone doesn't support it. World VR Mall's VR mode is built for the Meta Quest browser. Support changes, so check that live table before you rely on a specific device. On a phone or laptop it doesn't matter: the 3D mall runs without WebXR, and only the headset mode needs it.

Is a 3D mall worth it without a headset?

For most people, yes, because that's how most people use 3D anyway. In a 2026 survey of 400 consumers, 61% said they use a smartphone for immersive experiences (Clutch, see our 2026 AR and VR shopping numbers). A walk-in store on a phone screen still does the job a shop window does: it makes you stop and look. When you want to buy, the store's button sends you to its own checkout.

How do you try it in a minute?

  1. Open worldvrmall.com on whatever device you have.
  2. On a phone, drag to look and use the joystick or tap to walk; on a laptop, use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
  3. Walk up to a storefront, or pick one from the store directory and tap "Walk in" to land at its door.
  4. In a headset, open the same address in the headset's browser and choose Enter VR.

Is it comfortable in VR?

VR comfort varies from person to person. The mall has comfort options built in: a vignette that narrows your view while you move, snap turning, teleporting, and rides that keep you at seated eye height. Take breaks, play seated if you prefer, and stop if you feel unwell.

Is it safe for kids?

Anyone can explore the stores, rides and park solo, with no account and no ads. Live social features (seeing and hearing other people) are only for signed-in adults 18 and over with an allofus.one account; guests and under-18s never see or hear live people. Visitors under 13 who enter their age get a kids safe mode with no account, no camera, no live people and no forms. These rules are published in the mall's site facts file.

Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app to visit World VR Mall?

No. The mall runs in a web browser on phones, tablets, laptops and VR headsets. There is nothing to install and no account is needed to explore.

Does it work on an iPhone?

Yes, the 3D mall runs in a recent iPhone browser with on-screen controls. The VR headset mode is separate: Safari on iPhone does not support WebXR, so VR needs a headset browser such as the one on Meta Quest.

What if my device is slow?

Use the Mall feed or the store directory. They list every store, offer and product without loading the 3D engine, and every store has a Walk in button for when you are on a faster device.
