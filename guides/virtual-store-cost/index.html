What costs sit outside the storefront price?

A 3D storefront is a place to be found and browsed. It is not a checkout. We never take payments or hold customer data, so a shopper who taps "buy" lands on your own website. That means you still need one of these:

  • A store you already have. Shopify, Square, Etsy, WooCommerce or any site with a product page. Nothing extra to buy.
  • A hosted shop. For example, Shopify's Basic plan lists at $39 a month paid monthly, or $29 a month billed once yearly, with online card rates from 2.9% + 30¢ (Shopify pricing, checked November 22, 2026).
  • A phone number or booking link. Service businesses (painters, salons, auto glass) often send shoppers to a call or a quote form instead of a cart.

So a shop with no website at all, on a hosted plan paid monthly plus our basic storefront, would pay about $39 + $99 = $138 a month before card fees. A shop that already has a site pays $99.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

The rental page lists no setup fee, and storefronts rent month to month, so you can cancel. Setup is a phone call of about ten minutes. You send three things: your logo, your brand colors, and a product list with links to where people buy. The store is usually live the same day.

How does that compare with other ways to sell online?

The fair comparison isn't "3D store vs online store". You need the online store either way. The real question is whether a walk-in storefront brings enough extra attention to be worth $99. Online sales were 17.9% of all US retail sales in the third quarter of 2026, seasonally adjusted, so almost 18 cents of every retail dollar now goes through a screen (US Census Bureau, quarterly e-commerce report, released November 19, 2026). Standing out in that crowd is the hard part, and that is what a storefront is for.

Who gets the most out of a $99 storefront?

From the stores open today, three kinds of business fit best: shops with a small range of products that look good on a shelf, service businesses that want a memorable "walk-in" version of their brand (see Bear Creek Auto Glass), and brands that want a search-optimized store page of their own. If your products only make sense in a long filtered catalogue, a storefront is a showcase, not a replacement.

What we could not verify: whether a storefront raises your sales. That depends on your products and how you promote the store. We will publish real visitor numbers once we have enough months of data to be honest about them.

Questions people ask

Do I need a website to rent a 3D storefront?

You need somewhere for the buy button to go. That can be your own online store, a hosted shop, or for service businesses a phone number or booking page. World VR Mall never handles payment, so the sale and the customer data stay with you.

Can I cancel a storefront?

Yes. Store rentals are month to month. The 2026 founding rate is locked for 12 months from sign-up while you stay.

Will the price go up?

The 2026 founding rates are locked for 12 months from sign-up. Rates are expected to rise in 2027 for new sign-ups. Check the Rent a store page for the current list before you decide.
