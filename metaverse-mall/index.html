A metaverse mall that is just a website

No crypto wallet. No land to buy. No app. World VR Mall is a shared 3D place on the open web, where independent brands have walk-in storefronts and shoppers arrive from a link.

Visiting needs no account. If you want one, the same sign-in works on allofus.one, a sister world, and a Portal Hall inside the mall links to other WebXR worlds. Live features with other people follow the mall's safety rules.

For brands

A storefront in a metaverse mall usually means an agency quote. Here it is a month-to-month rental: $99 a month for a storefront with up to 8 products, set up by phone in about ten minutes. See virtual stores for small business.

How to get in

  • Phone or tablet: open worldvrmall.com, drag to look, use the joystick or tap where you want to walk.
  • Laptop or desktop: W-A-S-D or the arrow keys, drag the mouse to look.
  • VR headset (Meta Quest and other WebXR browsers): open the same address in the headset browser and tap Enter VR.
  • No 3D at all: the Mall feed and the Directory are plain pages with every store, product and phone number.
🛠️ Still being built. World VR Mall opened in 2026 and grows every week. Some corners look rough, especially in headsets. If something breaks, tell us; a person reads every report.

Questions

Do I need crypto or a wallet?

No. Nothing in World VR Mall uses crypto, tokens or NFTs.

Is this a game?

It is a mall with fun built in: coasters, an arcade and hidden things to find. Shopping always goes to real brands' real websites.

Can I visit without signing up?

Yes. Walking around and visiting stores needs no account.
