Scan a real store, build its mall twin

Open this page in the Quest 3 browser inside the store. The headset's own room understanding (floor, walls, ceiling, doors, tables, shelves) is read by this page and saved as a footprint file. We use that file to build the storefront in the mall to the real layout.

  1. On the Quest, finish Settings → Physical Space → Space Setup for the room first (one time; the headset walks the walls for you). Small rooms scan best.
  2. Tap Start scan below. Passthrough opens. Look around the room slowly for ten seconds.
  3. Pull the trigger (or tap Save) — the footprint is saved to your account and you can download or email it.
