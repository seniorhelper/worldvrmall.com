Inside World VR Mall: every wing, every store

You're standing in the Grand Lobby. The Americas Wing is to your left, Europe to your right, Asia · Africa · Islands straight ahead past the atrium. The Global Food Court is in the north-east corner, the Kids' Discovery Zone (giant slide, tube, kid train, ball pit, petting zoo, tic-tac-toe, writing wall) in the north-west, the Aquarium & Butterfly Garden with the waterfall lounge in the south-west, and the Sky Wheel in the south-east. Booths in the atrium, a glass elevator to the Cloud Deck, mall carts by the entrance, and a teleporter if your feet get tired. Green buttons appear when you're near something you can do.

New in Version 1.4: four more areas, 80 more storefronts

🏮 ChinatownThrough the portal at the end of the Europe Wing: a red-and-gold hall with paper lanterns, a paifang gate, a dragon overhead, a tea house that hands out fortunes, and 20 storefronts for tea, silk, jade, dumplings and more.
👽 Alien QuarterPast the Americas Wing: floating rocks, a hovering UFO whose tractor beam lifts you off the floor, alien shoppers, and 20 glow-lit storefronts for brands that are a little out there.
🤖 Future WingChrome floors, spinning holograms, robot shoppers, and 20 storefronts for gadgets, drones, VR gear and smart-home tech. Portal is just north of the atrium.
⬆️ Upper LevelTake the glass elevator or float up the flag-covered stairs. Twenty more stores, the World VR Cinema (two hours of bloopers, real seats, free popcorn), the Game Room with wall-sized tic-tac-toe, hangman, memory match and a reaction test, a grand piano you can play, and a roof that slides open.
🌇 Grand ConcourseWalk north past the Asia Wing and the mall simply keeps going: three levels of storefronts on both sides, bending away toward a sunset. Every space on it is available.
⏩ Moving walkways, sunroof, directory poleStand on a walkway and it carries you down the wing. The lobby roof retracts at the tap of a sign. The glowing directory pole by the entrance points the way to everything.
