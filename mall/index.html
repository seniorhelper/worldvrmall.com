Inside World VR Mall: every wing, every store

You're standing in the Grand Lobby. The Americas Wing is to your left, Europe to your right, Asia · Africa · Islands straight ahead past the atrium. The Global Food Court is in the north-east corner, the Kids' Discovery Zone (giant slide, tube, kid train, ball pit, petting zoo, tic-tac-toe, writing wall) in the north-west, the Aquarium & Butterfly Garden with the waterfall lounge in the south-west, and the Sky Wheel in the south-east. Booths in the atrium, a glass elevator to the Cloud Deck, mall carts by the entrance, and a teleporter if your feet get tired. Green buttons appear when you're near something you can do.
