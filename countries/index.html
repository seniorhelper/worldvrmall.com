How a country street works

  • Any brand, any currency. Buy buttons go to your own website. World VR Mall never handles the payment.
  • Your language and theirs. A store's clerk can greet in several languages; the mall uses the visitor's browser language and falls back to English. A shop from Shanghai can speak Mandarin, English, and Spanish.
  • Found two ways. Stores sit in regional wings and are cross-listed by category, so a Lagos fabric shop is next to its neighbors and also under "clothing."
  • Your street lights up. The World Pole in the atrium shows every country as its first store opens. First in gets remembered.

Bring your country's brands

One form for a single brand, a group of makers, a trade association, or a whole country's worth of stores. We'll reply with founding tenant rates and a plan.

Questions

Can a business from any country get a store in World VR Mall?

Yes. World VR Mall is built for brands from every country. Stores sit in regional wings and are cross-listed by category, and each country gets its own street as its first stores open. Buy buttons go to the brand's own website in any currency.

Can my store speak more than one language?

Yes. A store's clerk can greet and answer in several languages; the mall shows the visitor's browser language and falls back to English. A store from Shanghai can speak Mandarin, English, and Spanish.

How do I bring my country's brands to the mall?

Use the form on this page or call 1-800-481-8638. Founding tenant rates apply to sign-ups in 2026.
