How setup works

  1. Call 1-800-481-8638 or fill in the form on the rental page.
  2. Send your logo, colors and the products you want on the shelves (or point us at your website).
  3. We build the storefront. Most take about ten minutes on the phone.
  4. Share your store link. It opens right at your door in 3D, or in VR on a headset.

Who it suits, and who it doesn't

It suits small brands with products that look good on a shelf, local service businesses that want a memorable link, and makers who want to stand next to other independent shops instead of under a marketplace's search bar. It is not a replacement for your website or your store on a marketplace: it sends visitors to them.

Questions

No. It is month to month. 2026 sign-ups keep their rate for 12 months.

No. Buy buttons link to the product pages you already have.

Can customers visit in VR?

Yes. The same store opens in VR headsets with a WebXR browser, and on phones and laptops in 3D.
