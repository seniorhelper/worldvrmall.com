Why we built it

Online shopping won on convenience and lost on everything else. A grid of thumbnails has no front door, no neighbors, no music, nothing to discover. Small brands get buried under the biggest marketplaces on Earth, and shoppers never meet the people who actually make things.

We'd been building 3D walk-in websites for clients and kept thinking the same thought: if a single store can be a place, so can the whole mall. And if the mall is a place, it doesn't have to be in one city. It can have a New York store next to a Thai store next to a European store. A global melting pot of independent ecommerce.

Would you rather buy Christmas gifts from a grid, or walk into a mall with stores from all over the world?

What makes it different

🌍 Global by designWings are organized by region, not by category, so wandering feels like traveling. The food court is the world's kitchen.
🧑‍🍳 Buy from the sourceEvery buy button goes to the brand's own website. The mall introduces you; the maker sells to you. We never touch the payment.
🎢 Actually funCoasters, a Sky Wheel, a lake with a cruise ship, duck ponds, a train, a market with a street musician. Shopping is allowed to be a day out.

How to visit

  • Phone or tablet: open worldvrmall.com. Drag to look, pinch to zoom, use the joystick, or tap where you want to walk.
  • Laptop: W-A-S-D or arrow keys, drag the mouse to look, scroll to zoom in and out.
  • VR headset: open the same address in the headset browser and tap Enter VR.

Tip: tap the 🗺️ directory kiosk inside the atrium and it will walk you to any wing.

Privacy, in one paragraph

You can visit without an account: your character, your selfie and your shopping list then live in your own browser and never leave your device. Live social (seeing other people live, hugs, messages, voice) is only for signed-in adults 18+ with an allofus.one account (one login for both sites). If you sign in, your date of birth stays private and only decides that access; your name, avatar and position are shared with other signed-in adults near you; a selfie face is shown to others only after an automated safety check; voice is push-to-talk, peer to peer and never recorded. When the one-time age check for voice and video is switched on, only its result is kept, never your picture or your ID. Reports and the record of what was reported are kept securely for our safety team. If you send a bug report, we get what you write, the page you were on and your browser type, plus your email only if you add it. We don't run ads. When you buy something, you're on the brand's website, under their policies, not ours. Details: how we keep live social safe.

Questions

Who built World VR Mall?

World VR Mall is built and operated in Denver, Colorado by the team behind Eye To Ad Media, which has built search-optimized websites and keyword-domain networks since 2012.

Is World VR Mall safe for kids?

Kids can explore the stores, rides, park and games on their own, with no account. Live social features (seeing other people live, hugs, messages and voice) are only for signed-in adults 18+, so a child exploring the mall never sees or hears strangers. A child under 13 who enters their age gets kids safe mode on that device: no account, no camera, no live people and no forms. Everyone signed in can mute, block or report anyone, and a real person reviews every report. There are no ads, and purchases always happen on a brand's own website.

Does World VR Mall collect my data?

Not if you just visit: your character, selfie and shopping list stay in your own browser. If you sign in with an allofus.one account (adults 18+ only), your private date of birth decides access to live social; your name, avatar and position are shared only with other signed-in adults near you; a selfie face is shown to others only after an automated safety check; and voice is push-to-talk, peer to peer and never recorded. When the one-time age check for voice is switched on, we keep only its result, never your picture or your ID.

How do I visit in VR?

Open worldvrmall.com in the browser on a VR headset and tap Enter VR. The same mall you see on a phone becomes fully immersive. No app to install.
