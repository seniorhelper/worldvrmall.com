How World VR Mall works

Every store in the mall belongs to a real business. The storefront shows the brand's logo, colors, and products; the buy button links to that brand's own website, so your purchase, shipping, and support are handled by the people who make the product. World VR Mall is the place you discover them, side by side, the way you would in a real mall: a New York brand next to a Thai brand next to a European brand. That's the whole idea. A global melting pot of independent ecommerce, and a lot more fun than scrolling a grid of thumbnails.

Fun fact: The first indoor shopping mall in the United States opened in 1956 in Minnesota. World VR Mall opened in 2026, and it never closes, never has a parking problem, and lets you shop in your socks.

Explore the grounds

A world that floats

Pinch out or scroll back and the whole property turns out to be an island drifting above the clouds, rock and roots hanging beneath it. Drag to orbit it from any angle.

The hot-air balloon and the observatory

The balloon climbs to 190 meters on a slow two-minute circle, high enough to see every corner of the world and the sky panorama above it. The observatory on the far hill has a working telescope: point it at the mall, the coasters, the airport, or straight up at the night sky and distant things fill the screen.

Beach Cove and the World Zoo

At the far edge of the island: a beach with palms, umbrellas, a pier and a kickable ball, where the sky becomes a tropical ocean the moment you step on the sand. Next door, the World Zoo: lions, an elephant, giraffes, zebras, penguins and monkeys in six habitats, each with a fact sign and a feeding station. The zookeeper has opinions.

The park

Acres of green grass, walking paths, fountains, and floating affirmation banners. Sit by the lake, watch the cruise ship pass, or feed the ducks at either pond.

The amusement park

Three roller coasters, a ferris wheel, and a carousel. The Mall Blaster is the one that dives through the mall itself. Yes, you can watch it from inside.

The Old World Market

Cobblestone lanes, striped canopies, a gazebo, and a stone well. This is where handmade and homemade goods from small makers get their own booth. A viking boat sits at the market dock, because why not.

Train, airport, and teleport station

A high-speed train loops the whole property. A plane takes off from the airport on the far side of the park. And you arrived through the teleport station, which is where you'll land every time you come back.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a VR headset?

No. World VR Mall runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or laptop. If you visit from a headset browser, an Enter VR button appears automatically.

Is it free?

Visiting, exploring, riding the coasters, and building a shopping list are all free. Purchases happen on each brand's own site at their normal prices.

How do I move around?

On a phone: drag to look, pinch to zoom, use the joystick or D-pad, or tap where you want to walk. On a laptop: W-A-S-D or arrow keys, drag to look, scroll to zoom. In VR: left stick walks, right stick turns.

Is my selfie stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser on your own device. It's never sent to us or to any store.

How do I get my brand in the mall?

Call 1-800-481-8638. Setup takes about ten minutes. You'll pick a wing, choose your placement, and send a logo and product list. Your store goes live the same day.
