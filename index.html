What can you do in the 3D mall?

The mall sits on an island floating above the clouds. Inside are the shopping wings and a food court; outside is a park you can walk, ride and explore.

Three roller coasters

The Chiller, Aurora Loop and the Mall Blaster, which runs straight through the mall's glass towers. There is also a Sky Wheel and a carousel.

Shopping wings

A Grand Lobby, the Americas, Europe and Asia & Islands wings, a Global Food Court, a Kids' Discovery Zone, an Aquarium Garden and a Sky Deck.

Beach Cove and the World Zoo

A beach with palms and a pier, and a zoo with lions, an elephant, giraffes, zebras, penguins and monkeys, each with a fact sign.

Lake, ponds and the park

Walking paths and fountains, a lake with a cruise ship and two duck ponds.

The Old World Market

Cobblestone lanes and striped canopies where small makers sell handmade goods from their own booth.

Balloon, observatory, train

A hot-air balloon climbs to 190 meters, the observatory has a working telescope, and a train loops the island past the airport.

Shoppers can also put a selfie face on their character and keep one shopping list across every store. Both stay in your own browser.

How does renting a store work?

Any business can rent a walk-in storefront month to month. You send your logo, colors, products and the links to your own online store; setup takes about ten minutes by phone. Shoppers buy on your website, so the sale, the customer and the payment stay yours.

  • Storefront: $99 a month, up to 8 products, an AI clerk and buy buttons to your own site.
  • Storefront + Search Optimized: $199 a month, up to 25 products and a search-optimized store page at worldvrmall.com/stores/your-brand/.
  • These are founding rates for 2026 sign-ups, locked for 12 months. Premium placement near the entrance is limited.

Frequently asked questions

What is World VR Mall?

World VR Mall is a free virtual shopping mall with real stores. You can browse it on this page, walk through it in 3D in your browser, or step inside with a VR headset. Each store belongs to a real business, and its buy button goes to that business's own website, so payment, shipping and support stay with the people who sell the product. Around the mall is a park with roller coasters, a lake, a zoo, a beach and a market for makers.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. The 3D mall runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or laptop, with nothing to install. VR is an extra for headset browsers such as Meta Quest or Pico: open worldvrmall.com there and choose Enter VR. On a phone or tablet, VR would mean a split-screen cardboard view, so the site offers the 3D mall instead.

Is World VR Mall free to visit?

Yes. Browsing, walking the mall in 3D, riding the coasters and building a shopping list are all free, and you do not need an account. Anything you buy is bought on the brand's own website at its normal price. World VR Mall never takes your payment or your card details.

Why does the 3D mall take longer to open than this page?

The 3D mall has to download the island, the mall building, the rides and the store shelves before it can draw them, which takes a while on a first visit and on slow connections. This page is plain text and links, so it opens at once. Browse here first if you are in a hurry, then choose Walk in 3D when you want the full world. Later visits start faster because your browser keeps the files.

How does a business get a store in World VR Mall?

Call 1-800-481-8638. A storefront takes about ten minutes to set up: your logo, colors, products and the links to your own online store. Founding rates for 2026 start at $99 a month for a storefront with up to 8 products, or $199 a month with up to 25 products and a search-optimized store page. Storefronts are rented month to month. Full details are on the Rent a store page.
